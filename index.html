# Privacy Policy — Mobile Lab X **Last updated: November 12, 2026** This Privacy Policy describes how the **Mobile Lab X** Android application ("the App") handles information. It applies to the App only and describes **only features that actually exist in the App**. The App does **not** require or create user accounts. ## 1. Internet Usage The App requires an active internet connection and uses it to: - Fetch the public phone-specifications database over HTTPS from `raw.githubusercontent.com`. - Load device images over HTTPS from `fdn2.gsmarena.com`. The App only loads image URLs from this verified host; URLs from other hosts are ignored. - Load and serve advertisements through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), as described in §2. The full catalog is fetched from the network whenever it is loaded; the App does not keep a full catalog cache for offline browsing and cannot display the catalog offline. For optional catalog change notifications, the App stores a compact local snapshot and notification metadata as described in §4. The servers receive your IP address and standard request metadata (including the requested URL and user agent). The App does not send favorites, notification preferences, language or appearance preferences, local notification history, contacts, or files to these servers. ## 2. Advertising The App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are loading or shown, **Google**—not the App—receives and processes data such as your IP address, device identifiers (including, where available and permitted, the Google advertising ID), device characteristics, and approximate location derived from IP, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy and terms: - Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads - Google Ads & Technology settings (turn off ad personalization for your account): https://adssettings.google.com Ad formats in a release may include app-open, banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. App-open ads are served only when a valid app-open ad unit is configured for that release; otherwise this format is disabled. Rewarded ads are shown only after you explicitly choose to watch them. In regions where Google's privacy messaging requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), the App shows Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form** before ad serving begins, and honors the consent choice you make there. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the **"Privacy options"** entry in the App's Settings screen (shown where required), or via Google Ads Settings linked above. The App does not itself store, export, or sell any advertising profile. Outside consent regions, the App's data collection remains as described in §3. ## 3. Data Collection The App has no account system and does not include app-side analytics or tracking. Beyond the advertising data processed by Google as described in §2, the App does not request access to: - Location (GPS). - Camera or microphone. - Contacts, call logs, SMS, or phone state. ## 4. Local Storage The App stores data **on your device only**: - Your app preferences (language, dark mode, favorite device IDs, and whether app notifications are enabled). - A compact notification snapshot containing public device IDs and hashes of selected public specification fields, used to detect catalog changes. - Notification metadata needed to prevent duplicate notifications, including pending event device IDs, brand/model, event type, timestamp and status, processed event IDs, and the last-send time. - The last automated notification type, used to rotate notification content. - The UMP consent-decision record kept by the Google Mobile Ads SDK when required by your region. The full catalog is held for the current app session and is not cached to disk; the compact notification snapshot described above is stored locally. The App disables Android backup (`android:allowBackup="false"`). Local app data is removed when you uninstall the App; there is no in-app clear-cache control. ## 5. Permissions Summary | Permission | Used for | | --- | --- | | `INTERNET` | Fetching the public specs database and device images, and ad delivery | | `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Checking for a validated internet connection before fetching | | `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Posting the app's own optional catalog-change and device/game-exploration notifications on Android 13+ (granted only if you approve the system dialog) | These are the three permissions declared directly by the App. The final merged Android package also includes permissions contributed by Google Mobile Ads and AndroidX WorkManager, including advertising ID and Android Ad Services permissions, plus permissions used for background work (such as `WAKE_LOCK` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`). Ad-related processing is described in §2. ## 6. Children's Privacy The App is a reference tool for phone specifications and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Network providers and the advertising SDK still receive the connection metadata described above. ## 7.
