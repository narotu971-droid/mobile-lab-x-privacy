Privacy Policy for Mobile Lab X

Effective Date: May 24, 2024
Last Updated: May 24, 2024
Application Name: Mobile Lab X
Package Name: com.aistudio.phonefps.wkyz

1. Overview

Mobile Lab X ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our privacy practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Mobile Lab X is an informational smartphone specification, comparison, and gaming hardware estimation tool. The App is designed to function without requiring user registration, personal logins, or the input of personal data.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Data

We do not collect, request, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII)—such as your name, email address, phone number, contact list, real-time geographic location, or identity information.

B. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a customized user experience, Mobile Lab X saves certain preference data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any external party:

  • UI Preferences: Dark Mode toggle and selected language (English / Arabic).
  • Notification Preferences: Your preference regarding automated local device notifications.
  • Favorites: A list of device identifiers for smartphones you have bookmarked.

3. Android System Permissions Requested

Mobile Lab X requests only the minimum required Android system permissions necessary to deliver its features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch publicly hosted device specification data, load phone product images, and serve advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check whether your device has an active internet connection before initiating network requests.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Android 13 / API 33 and higher) Used to post optional local notifications on your device regarding catalog updates. You can enable or disable notifications at any time via the App’s settings or your device system settings.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To maintain the App as a free service, Mobile Lab X integrates third-party SDKs for network connectivity, image loading, and advertising display. These third parties may collect non-personal device information as described in their respective privacy policies.

A. Google AdMob & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements within the App, and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request user consent for personalized or non-personalized advertising where required by law (such as in the EEA and UK).

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • IP address (used to infer approximate country/city level location for ad serving).
  • Technical diagnostic data, performance metrics, and ad interaction data.

For users in regions subject to GDPR/CCPA regulations, you are presented with a consent form powered by Google UMP upon launching the App. You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time inside the App by visiting Settings > Privacy Options.

B. Data and Asset Providers

  • GitHub Raw (raw.githubusercontent.com): The App fetches public phone specification data hosted on GitHub over secure HTTPS connections.
  • GSMArena (fdn2.gsmarena.com): Product images are dynamically retrieved from GSMArena’s content delivery network over secure HTTPS connections.

5. Children's Privacy

Mobile Lab X does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain jurisdictions). The App is intended for general audiences interested in technology and smartphone specifications. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a third-party service, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Data Security

Because Mobile Lab X does not collect or transmit personal user data to external servers, your data privacy is inherently protected on your device. Network requests for device specifications and images are securely transmitted using encrypted HTTPS protocols.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
